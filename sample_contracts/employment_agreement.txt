EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on January 1, 2024, between Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("Company") and Rajesh Kumar ("Employee").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
The Employee is hired as a Senior Software Engineer and agrees to perform duties as assigned by the Company. The Employee shall devote full-time attention to the business of the Company.

2. COMPENSATION
2.1 The Employee shall be paid a monthly salary of ₹1,20,000, subject to applicable tax deductions.
2.2 The Employee may be eligible for an annual performance bonus at the sole discretion of the Company.

3. TERMINATION
3.1 The Company may terminate this Agreement at any time without cause by providing 30 days' written notice.
3.2 If the Employee terminates employment without providing 90 days' notice, they shall pay the Company a penalty equivalent to three months' salary.

4. NON-COMPETE CLAUSE
The Employee agrees not to engage in any business that competes with the Company for a period of 24 months following termination of employment, within a radius of 50 kilometers from any Company office.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work product, inventions, and intellectual property created by the Employee during employment, whether during working hours or not, shall be the exclusive property of the Company in perpetuity.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Employee agrees to maintain confidentiality of all Company information in perpetuity, even after termination of employment.

7. INDEMNIFICATION
The Employee shall indemnify and hold harmless the Company from any claims, damages, or losses arising from the Employee's actions or omissions during employment.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any disputes arising from this Agreement shall be resolved exclusively through arbitration in Mumbai, with the Company having the right to choose the arbitrator.

9. AUTO-RENEWAL
This Agreement automatically renews for successive one-year terms unless either party provides 180 days' notice of non-renewal.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, with jurisdiction in Mumbai courts only.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement on the date first written above.
