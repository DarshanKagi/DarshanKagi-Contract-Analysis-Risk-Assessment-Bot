COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on March 1, 2024, between Property Holdings Ltd. ("Landlord") and Startup Innovations Pvt. Ltd. ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES
Landlord leases to Tenant the commercial property located at 123 Business Park, Bangalore, consisting of approximately 2,000 square feet ("Premises").

2. TERM
The initial lease term is five (5) years, commencing April 1, 2024, and ending March 31, 2029.

3. RENT
3.1 Monthly rent is ₹2,50,000, payable in advance on the first day of each month.
3.2 Rent shall increase by 15% annually on each anniversary date.
3.3 Late payment shall incur a penalty of ₹10,000 per day.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall pay a security deposit of ₹15,00,000, which may be used by Landlord for any reason Landlord deems appropriate.

5. USE OF PREMISES
Tenant may use the Premises only for purposes approved in writing by Landlord. Landlord may withdraw such approval at any time without notice.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Tenant shall be responsible for all maintenance, repairs, and improvements to the Premises, including structural repairs and common area maintenance.

7. TERMINATION
7.1 Landlord may terminate this Lease at any time with 60 days' notice for any reason or no reason.
7.2 If Tenant terminates the Lease before the end of the term, Tenant shall pay the remaining rent for the entire lease term plus a penalty of ₹10,00,000.

8. RENEWAL
This Lease automatically renews for successive five-year terms unless Tenant provides 365 days' written notice of non-renewal.

9. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING
Tenant shall not assign or sublet the Premises without Landlord's written consent, which Landlord may withhold for any reason.

10. INDEMNIFICATION
Tenant shall indemnify and hold Landlord harmless from all claims, including those arising from Landlord's own negligence or willful misconduct.

11. INSURANCE
Tenant shall maintain comprehensive liability insurance with coverage of at least ₹1,00,00,000 naming Landlord as primary beneficiary.

12. DEFAULT
Default by Tenant (including any delay in rent of more than 3 days) permits Landlord to immediately re-enter the Premises and seize all Tenant property without further notice.

13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration with an arbitrator selected by Landlord.

14. MODIFICATIONS
Tenant may not make any modifications to the Premises. All fixtures and improvements made by Tenant become Landlord's property immediately.

Executed on the date first written above.
