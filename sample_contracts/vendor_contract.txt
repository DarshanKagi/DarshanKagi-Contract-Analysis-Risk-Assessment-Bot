VENDOR SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Vendor Service Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective as of February 15, 2024, by and between Bright Retail Pvt. Ltd. ("Client") and Quality Supplies Co. ("Vendor").

1. SERVICES
Vendor agrees to provide inventory management and logistics services as specified in Schedule A, attached hereto.

2. TERM
The initial term of this Agreement shall be for three (3) years, commencing on March 1, 2024.

3. PAYMENT TERMS
3.1 Client shall pay Vendor ₹5,00,000 per month for services rendered.
3.2 Late payments shall incur a penalty of 3% per month.
3.3 All payments are non-refundable under any circumstances.

4. PERFORMANCE STANDARDS
Vendor shall maintain service levels as reasonably determined by Client from time to time. Failure to meet performance standards may result in immediate termination.

5. LIABILITY
5.1 Vendor shall be liable for any and all losses, damages, or claims arising from services, without limitation.
5.2 Vendor agrees to indemnify Client against all claims, lawsuits, and expenses of any nature.

6. TERMINATION
Client may terminate this Agreement immediately without cause or prior notice. Upon termination by Client, Vendor shall pay liquidated damages of ₹20,00,000.

7. EXCLUSIVITY
Vendor agrees not to provide similar services to any other retail businesses during the term of this Agreement and for 18 months thereafter.

8. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
This Agreement will automatically renew for successive two-year periods unless Vendor provides written notice of non-renewal at least 180 days before the end of the current term.

9. AMENDMENTS
Client reserves the right to modify the terms of this Agreement at any time, with changes becoming effective upon written notice to Vendor.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Delhi. Vendor waives any right to appeal decisions.

11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Any intellectual property, including but not limited to processes, systems, or inventions developed during the provision of services, shall belong exclusively to Client.

12. FORCE MAJEURE
Client shall not be liable for any failure to perform due to circumstances beyond its reasonable control. However, Vendor remains obligated to perform regardless of force majeure events.
